What “pre-token” means here

A protocol counts as pre-token when its record carries no token — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is what the record says, not proof it has none: a protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry. The board never predicts whether or when a token is issued; the implied value is the entry's own claim about what one would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is.

Reading a farm plan

Each plan is seven steps derived from the protocol's category, chains, TVL and age. Every step states what it costs and how long it takes, with gas budgeted for the chain it runs on. Nothing here is financial advice, and no step ever asks you to pay to claim anything.
